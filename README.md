# Support Macro Pad — CX Workflow, Refund Calculator & QA Compliance Workbench

**Live Dashboard:** [Support Macro Pad Workbench](https://stephenkrolikowskiud.github.io/MacroPad/)

**Author:** Stephen Krolikowski ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephenkrolikowski))

## 1. Executive Overview

The Support Macro Pad is a zero-dependency, browser-based Customer Experience (CX) workbench designed from frontline high-volume support operational experience and rapidly prototyped using **AI-Assisted Development (OpenAI Codex)**. Built to eliminate repetitive math, manual template searching, and compliance errors, this tool streamlines agent workflow, reduces Average Handle Time (AHT), and enforces regulatory brand standards across thousands of customer interactions.

## 2. Key Product & Operational Features

- **128 Categorized Response Macros (8 Banks × 16 Pads):** Pre-configured template response banks covering Withdrawals & Refunds, Verification & Bans, Promo & Bonus Playthrough, Tax & 1099s, Payment Declines, Pick'em / Streaks / Ladders, Technical Troubleshooting, and VIP Outreach.
- **Automated Playthrough & Refund Calculator:** Embedded financial calculation engine that automatically splits multi-part deposits into refundable cash vs. playthrough-locked promotional balances (e.g., calculating exact `$X` refundable cash alongside `$Y` remaining playthrough requirement).
- **Real-Time Regulatory Voice Linter:** Live compliance auditing engine that monitors agent response text, instantly flagging banned terms (such as `guarantee`, `wager`, or `gamble`), passive voice constructions, and exclamation inflation before responses are sent to customers.
- **120-Day Local Telemetry & Optimization:** Privacy-first local analytics layer tracking active usage days, total fires, and unused macro pads, empowering operations leads to prune underperforming macros and optimize template layouts based on empirical data.
- **Optional WebMIDI Hardware Integration:** Direct physical macro mapping support for hardware controllers like the Native Instruments Maschine Mikro Mk3, as well as universal keyboard hotkey shortcuts (`1–8` for banks, `A–H` for pads).

## 3. Data Sanitization & Governance Notice

All proprietary brand names, company-specific terminology, internal knowledge base links, and policy URLs have been sanitized and replaced with generic portfolio placeholders (e.g., `Apex Fantasy` and `example.com`) to ensure full compliance with confidentiality standards while maintaining operational fidelity.
